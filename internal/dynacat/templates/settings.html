    Add a person

    Signing in as any of these signs in as this person. Use it for someone's Tinyauth username when it differs from their username here, or to fold a second account into this one, such as linking a local account to an admin. Linking a name that has its own profile replaces that profile.

    Changing the username keeps their profile, password, theme, notes, to-dos and page access. They sign in with the new name from now on. If they sign in through Tinyauth or another sign-in service, change it there too.

    Sign in with Tinyauth, or another forward-auth proxy such as Authelia or Authentik. The proxy checks who someone is and tells DashDen in request headers. DashDen only believes those headers when the request comes straight from an address you trust.

    Sign-in button (OIDC)

    Your household's own apps. Connect one, test it, and add its widget to a page. Addresses and tokens are saved as secrets, so they never end up in the config file.

    Den theme pack

    Pick the themes you like and add them. They show up in the theme picker, can be given to people on the People tab, and can be changed with the pencil like any other theme.

    The theme picker is hidden (Server tab), so people can't choose these themselves. Household and seasonal themes still work.

    Seasonal themes

    Change the dashboard's default theme for part of the year. Dates are month-day and can wrap past New Year, like 12-20 to 01-06. Anyone who picked a theme for themselves keeps it.
